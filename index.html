Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before you start.

How does Smart Cleanup find duplicate photos?

Smart Cleanup analyzes your photo library on your device and compares images to detect exact duplicates and visually similar shots. It then groups them together so you can quickly pick the best photo to keep and remove the rest.

Will deleting photos free up storage on my iPhone?

Yes. Removing duplicate photos, similar images, and old screenshots frees up real storage space. Deleted items move to your "Recently Deleted" album, so you can empty that album to reclaim the space immediately.

Is Smart Cleanup safe? Could I lose important photos?

Smart Cleanup never deletes anything automatically. You always review the suggested items and confirm before anything is removed, so your important memories stay safe.

Do my photos ever leave my device?

No. All scanning and analysis happen entirely on your iPhone. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to any server, keeping your data completely private.

Is Smart Cleanup free to download?

Yes, Smart Cleanup is free to download on the App Store. Some advanced features may be available through an optional subscription managed by Apple.

Which devices are supported?

Smart Cleanup is designed and optimized for iPhone and works with your standard iOS Photos library. Simply download it from the App Store to get started.
